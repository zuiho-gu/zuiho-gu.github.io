About

About Me

I hold a Master's degree in Computer Science and Technology from Minzu University of China, where I achieved an average score of 92/100 (Rank: 2/16). My research focuses on AI game intelligence — applying reinforcement learning and large language models to board game decision-making.

My work spans from developing unified decision-making frameworks for two-player board games to exploring how generative AI can support the preservation of Tibetan chess as an intangible cultural heritage.

I've published 3 papers (accepted) and have 2 more under review. I was also the principal contributor to NSFC and Tibet Autonomous Region science program proposals.

Research

Publications

CAAI Transactions on Intelligence Technology · Feb. 2026

MyGO: Multitype Game Optimisation — A Two-Stage Fine-Tuning Framework for Multi-Game Optimisation With Large Language Models

Proposes a unified structural encoding method (ASU) for heterogeneous game representation and a Token-level Adaptive PPO algorithm for reinforcement learning under sparse rewards. Demonstrates superior strategy quality and rule generalization across multiple board games.

Accepted
Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence · Feb. 2026

MJ_RM: Efficient Mahjong Model Using Improved Distributed PPO and Res2Net-LSTM Network

A reinforcement learning model for Chinese Standard Mahjong combining improved Distributed PPO with multi-stage rewards and a Res2Net-LSTM architecture. Demonstrates cross-game generalization on Gomoku and Dou Dizhu.

Accepted
Journal of Chongqing University of Technology · Aug. 2025

A Survey of Computer Game Research on Tibetan Jiu Chess

A comprehensive review of AI approaches for Tibetan Jiu Chess, analyzing expert-knowledge methods and deep reinforcement learning algorithms, with discussion on efficient gameplaying under resource constraints.
